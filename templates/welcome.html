WELCOME TO OUR BIOREACTOR CONTROL PAGE!

On the Stirring page, you can set the speed of the mixer. The accompanying line graph shows the average stirring speed measured during the last minute in 10-second blocks. This helps ensure your materials are being mixed consistently.

Use this page to control the system's temperature. You can enter a new temperature setting, which is then sent to the equipment. The graph tracks the average temperature trend over the last 60 seconds, helping you keep the environment stable.

This page lets you set the target acidity/alkalinity (PH) for your system. It displays a line graph showing the average PH level over the last minute. This is the best place to monitor and control your solution's quality.
